2.Hrudhayam Murali

Murali is a young man who values love, friendship, and the beautiful moments of life. His journey is filled with emotions, dreams, and memories that shape his personality. He believes that true happiness comes from the people who stand by us and the experiences we share with them. Through every challenge and joyful moment, Murali continues to move forward with hope, love, and a positive heart.

3.Nagabandham

Nagabandham is an intriguing story that blends mystery, mythology, adventure, and ancient secrets. The story takes the audience on a thrilling journey filled with hidden mysteries, powerful legends, and unexpected discoveries. As the characters uncover the secrets behind the Nagabandham, they are drawn into a world where the past and present come together. With its mysterious atmosphere, emotional moments, and adventurous storyline, Nagabandham promises to be an engaging cinematic experience.

4.Srinivasa Mangapuram

Srinivasa Mangapuram is a famous spiritual and historical place near Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, known for the Sri Kalyana Venkateswara Swamy Temple. The temple is dedicated to Lord Venkateswara and is believed to be associated with the divine marriage of Lord Venkateswara and Goddess Padmavathi. Surrounded by a peaceful and devotional atmosphere, the temple attracts thousands of devotees throughout the year. It is an important pilgrimage destination and a beautiful place to experience the spiritual and cultural heritage of the Tirupati region.

5.Oh Bamma Ayyo Rama

Oh Bhama Ayyo Rama is an emotional Telugu romantic drama that beautifully combines love, family, comedy, and the passion for cinema. The story revolves around Ram, a young man who dreams of becoming a film director and whose life is deeply influenced by his mother. His journey takes an emotional turn when Satyabhama enters his life and brings love and hope back into his world. With its blend of romance, family emotions, humour, and filmmaking, the movie presents a heart-touching story about love, dreams, and relationships.
